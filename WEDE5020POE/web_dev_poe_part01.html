About Clinix

Providing free and affordable medical care to orphans and less fortunate families in Durban since 2020.

Founded in 2020, Clinix began as a small mobile clinic serving township communities in Durban. Today we operate a permanent facility focused on children, orphans, and low income families.

Our Services

All services are free for orphans and offered on a pay what u can basis for other low income patients.

  • General Health Check ups:Basic consultations and screenings
  • Pediatric Care:Child wellness, immunizations, and nutrition support
  • HIV Testing & Counselling:Fee, confidential testing and support groups
  • Mental Health Support:Counselling for orphans and trauma survivors
  • Community Outreach:Mobile clinics to schools and orphanages

Mission & Vision

  • Mission:To provide compassionate, accessible healthcare to the less fortunate and orphans.
  • Vision:A South Africa where every child has access to quality health and wellness services
